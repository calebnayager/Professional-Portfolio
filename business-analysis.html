FleetPro Logistics Management System

Status: In Progress

FleetPro Logistics Management System is a Business Analysis and Systems Development project focused on improving the management of vehicle maintenance within a growing fleet.

The project is based on a real-world business problem where maintenance information was managed across spreadsheets, paper records, invoices, and communication channels.

Business Problem

The project investigates how a growing fleet can improve the way vehicle maintenance information is captured, updated, stored, verified, and accessed.

The aim is to replace fragmented information management with a more controlled and centralised process.

Project Approach

I am applying a structured Business Analysis and Software Development Life Cycle approach.

The project progresses from understanding the business problem and current processes through requirements, solution evaluation, systems analysis, development, testing, and implementation.

Business Analysis Work

  • Business problem identification and investigation
  • Root-cause analysis
  • Business requirements
  • As-Is process analysis
  • To-Be process design
  • Gap analysis
  • Solution evaluation
  • Systems analysis and requirements definition
