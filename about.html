About Me

I am a IT professional with a passion for software development, business and systems analysis, and cybersecurity. I enjoy transforming ideas and real-world problems into practical, technology-driven solutions while understanding both the technical and business requirements behind them. With a strong foundation in IT and hands-on project experience, I’m continuously expanding my skills, challenging myself with new technologies, and looking for opportunities to create meaningful solutions, solve problems, and grow within the technology industry.

My Journey

My interest in technology began at a young age, when I became fascinated by robotics engineering, and automation. At around 12 years old, I started exploring technologies such as Arduino and learning how they could be used to build and automate systems. That early curiosity sparked my interest in understanding how technology works and how different components can be combined to create practical solutions.

Over time, that interest developed into a broader passion for Information Technology, leading me to pursue formal studies and develop knowledge across software development, IT systems, business and systems analysis, and cybersecurity. Through my studies and personal projects, I have continued turning that early curiosity into hands-on experience, building websites and software applications while exploring different areas of technology.

Today, I approach technology with the same curiosity that first drew me to robotics: understanding how things work, solving problems, building solutions, and continuously learning what's next.
